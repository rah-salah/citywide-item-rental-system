Our story

Built in Jigjiga, for Jigjiga

Citywide Jigjiga is a small, local team turning idle gear into income — and giving neighbours access to what they need, when they need it.

Trust first

Every lender and renter is verified. Payments are held in escrow until handoff is complete.

Local by design

Built around Jigjiga neighbourhoods — Jigjiga Sefer, Sheik Ali Jowhar, Kebele 01–10, Awale Area, Dhagaxle Area, Jigjiga Central — so pickups are quick and easy.

Open to all

From students renting cameras to families lending wedding gear — Citywide is for everyone in Jigjiga.

Jigjiga's trusted marketplace for renting and sharing items locally.
